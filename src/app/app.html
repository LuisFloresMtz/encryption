How It Works

Cesar Cipher: Shifts each letter in the alphabet by a fixed number (deplacement).

For example, with a deplacement of 3: A → D, B → E, C → F, ... , X → A, Y → B, Z → C.

Atbash Cipher: Replaces each letter with its opposite in the alphabet

For example: A ↔ Z, B ↔ Y, C ↔ X, ... , M ↔ N.

Non-alphabetic characters remain unchanged.
